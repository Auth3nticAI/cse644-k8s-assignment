=== [C] Readiness vs liveness ===========================================
Liveness (/healthz)  : is the process itself able to answer at all?
                       No external dependency - a stalled disk or a
                       missing Secret must NOT cause a restart loop.
Readiness (/readyz)  : are this Pod's real dependencies in place -
                       PVC mounted and writable, ConfigMap greeting
                       set, Secret loaded? If not, Kubernetes should
                       stop sending it traffic (pull it from the
                       Service's Endpoints) without killing it.
